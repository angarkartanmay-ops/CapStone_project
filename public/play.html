        How to play

        Goal. Be first to empty your hand.

        On your turn play a card that matches the top card's suit or number, or an action that matches its action. A prism plays on anything.

        Spill. After a number, keep adding numbers of the same suit that climb or fall by one: 4, 5, 6. Select them in order, then press Play. Three or more cards make the next player draw one.

        Can't or won't play? Draw one. If it fits you may play it, otherwise your turn ends.

        Surge next player draws two, unless they surge back. Eddy reverses play. Gift hand one of your cards to the next player.

        Keys: click cards to select · Enter plays · D draws · P passes · Esc clears
